Stretching the Truth

Uncommercial project made with friends on GMTK Game Jam in 2024

Overview

Game Jam Theme was : SCALE

Small giraffe wants to resolve the conflict between the flatness and the roundness of the Earth. It finds a delicious NECKtarine 🍊, that makes giraffe grow its neck everytime it eats. Giraffe thinks that if its neck will be high enough, it will find out, if Earth is flat or round. The giraffe’s neck keeps growing, lifting it higher and higher towards the sky, where it begins to uncover the truth about Earth — and encounters a sinister Sun determined to keep that Truth hidden.

Can you defeat the final boss and find out the Truth?


Fun fuct:

This game was essentially an experiment, we made a small challenge to create this game without having any experience with Godot engine before. Eventually, we managed to make a working game in 48 hours. It was a lot of fun and satisfaction to create something in an unfamilliar engine. ☺️

Features

  • Floating objects

    All objects that float from right to left. The giraffe can eat them to gain a small amount of points. Objects with a red outline are too large for the giraffe to eat and will hit the giraffe upon collision.

  • Giraffe's neck expansion

    Every time the giraffe eats an object, its neck grows longer and longer. Each object it eats gives the giraffe points. Once it reaches a certain score, the giraffe grows and becomes able to eat larger objects.

  • Boss fight

    At the end of the game, the giraffe encounters the Sun, which, like the other objects, is initially too large for the giraffe to eat. To defeat the Sun, the giraffe must continue growing. After several growth stages, the giraffe becomes large enough to eat the Sun, bringing the game to an end.
